Q3. Does it take the same amount of work to compute `isPerfect` on the number 10 as it does on the number 1000? Why or why not? Does it take the same amount of work for `findPerfects` to search the range of numbers from 1-1000 as it does to search the numbers from 1000-2000? Why or why not?
A3. It doesn't take the same amount of work to compute 10 versus 1000. This is because 10 only needs to check all numbers from 1 - 9 as divisors. 1000 would need to check numbers from 1 - 999 as divisors, which will take much longer. Additionally, searching the range of numbers from 1-1000 is still faster than
numbers 1000 - 2000. This is because searching 1 - 1000 will only take divisors from 1 to 1000, however searching 1000-2000 also starts the divisor count at 1, but will eventually pass 1000 up until 2000.
